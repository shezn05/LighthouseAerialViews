Lighthouse displays modelled weather conditions and daily solar times. Temperature
values are rounded, WMO weather codes are converted to descriptions/icons, and
solar times are formatted for display. Data is not a house-level station reading.

API documentation: https://open-meteo.com/en/docs
Geocoding: https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/geocoding-api
