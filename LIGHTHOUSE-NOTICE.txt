Lighthouse Aerial Views
Independent modified version maintained by shezn05.
Initial modifications: 2 November 2026.

Based on Aerial Views 1.8.5 by Neil Turner:
https://github.com/theothernt/AerialViews
Originally based on Aerial Dream by Daniel Cachapa:
https://github.com/cachapa/AerialDream

Modifications add a distinct application ID, name, original lighthouse artwork,
Projectivy identity, attribution and custom build/signing configuration. The custom
flavor uses upstream's existing telemetry-free helper. Playback and screensaver
implementation are preserved. No affiliation with or endorsement by the original
authors is implied.
